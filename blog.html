Why talkingmind.cc isn't "voice-based therapy"

Scope

talkingmind.cc's original pitch described a voice-based therapy and coaching platform with clinical protocols and insurance billing. That framing carries real liability - licensed therapy and clinical treatment are regulated practices, and an AI chatbot standing in for either is a genuine harm risk for people actually in crisis, not just an overclaim.

The product that's actually built and live is scoped deliberately smaller and safer: a mood check-in that always surfaces real crisis resources (988, Crisis Text Line) regardless of score, plus a personalized AI-generated reflection/journaling prompt and follow-up question - text only, no voice, no diagnosis, no treatment claim. See it live at talkingmind.cc.
