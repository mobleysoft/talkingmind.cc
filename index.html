How it works

  • 1. Visit talkingmind.cc and log a quick mood score, no signup required.
  • 2. Optionally name a topic (work, a relationship, a decision) for a personalized prompt.
  • 3. Get a real, freshly-generated reflection question and follow-up to journal on.
  • 4. Every visit surfaces real crisis resources (988, Crisis Text Line) regardless of your mood score - unconditionally, not just when a score looks low.

talkingmind.cc is a journaling and self-reflection tool, not a therapist, not a diagnosis, and not crisis care. If you're in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or text HOME to 741741. For ongoing support, see a licensed provider - the live site links to real public directories (Open Path Collective, Psychology Today's therapist finder).
